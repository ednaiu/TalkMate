## 6. Automated analysis and sensitive information

Results may be inaccurate and are not intended for legally or similarly significant decisions. Emotion estimates, if available, are probabilistic inferences, not diagnoses or certain knowledge of internal states.

A voice recording is not automatically special-category biometric data merely because it contains a voice; processing technique and identification purpose matter. The examined function does not identify speakers. Content and inferences may nevertheless reveal sensitive information and require protection.

Emotion inference requires a separate EU AI Act assessment. Certain workplace and educational-institution uses are prohibited; consent cannot override that prohibition. A privacy notice alone does not validate deployment.

The examined code does not automatically add user recordings to model training. Any future training purpose would require separate assessment, information and a proper basis; this draft does not authorize it.

## 8. Your rights

Applicable rights include information, access, correction, erasure or cessation of unlawful processing. GDPR also provides restriction, objection and portability where its conditions are met. Withdrawal of consent is prospective and does not invalidate prior lawful processing or another proper basis.

Requests: [CONTACT]. Identity checks must be proportionate, not automatic passport demands. GDPR ordinarily requires a response within one month; any lawful extension needs appropriate notice. Russian statutory periods apply to Russian requests rather than automatically using the EU period.

You can complain to CNIL in France, another competent EU authority under the applicable rules, or Roskomnadzor in Russia. Contacting the operator first does not remove the right to complain to an authority.

## 9. Security, permissions and age

The examined iOS client stores its token in Keychain; the server stores password hashes. These measures do not guarantee absolute security. Public deployment needs protected transport, access controls, backups and incident handling. Development permits HTTP and must not be described as a secured public configuration.

Microphone permission can be withdrawn in iPhone settings; recording then becomes unavailable, but document reading does not require it. Local preferences support the chosen language and server. Optional analytics or advertising require reassessment before activation.

The proposed initial audience is 18+. This is a product choice, not a claim that GDPR always requires age 18. A minors process is not established here.
