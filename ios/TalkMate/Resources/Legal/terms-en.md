## 2. Purpose and age

Talk Mate supports personal speech rehearsal: recording, optional PDFs, transcripts, metrics, recommendations and practice history. Age 18+ is a proposed product boundary, not a universal legal age for every online service. Access by minors would require a separate process not established here.

The app is not medical care, psychotherapy or a diagnosis tool. It does not identify a person by voice or promise to eliminate anxiety. It does not replace professional assistance.

## 3. Access and your account

Protect your password, use only your own account and report unauthorized access to the published support contact. Recording requires microphone permission; server analysis requires a working connection and a trusted configured server. A separately chosen third-party server requires checking its operator and processing terms.

## 4. Your recordings and slides

You retain your rights to your materials. The provider needs only the limited technical rights necessary to host, reproduce and perform the requested analysis. This does not authorize selling, publishing or using your materials for advertising. Personal-data retention and deletion remain subject to the privacy notice and applicable law.

Upload only material you are entitled to use. Do not record other people or include confidential, sensitive or third-party data without an appropriate basis. Uploading does not authorize model training or public sharing.

## 5. Automated results and permitted use

Recognition and metrics may be wrong because of noise, hardware, language, accent and other factors. Interpret advice in context. Progress messages describe the purpose of practice, not a guarantee that every attempt will improve or a presentation will succeed.

Any emotion estimate is a model inference, not a reliable finding of your inner state. Do not use results for recruitment, educational selection, disciplinary action, medical decisions or other decisions about significant rights. EU AI Act prohibitions are not waived by agreement or consent. Emotion-related functionality requires a separate legal assessment before EU deployment.

Do not access others’ data, bypass controls, attack the service or upload unlawful content. Proportionate restrictions may address established abuse or security threats; where lawful and feasible, reasons and a route to challenge are provided. Refusing optional processing is not itself abuse.
